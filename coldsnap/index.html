How a project works

You work with me directly, from the first call to long after launch. No agency or account manager in between.

  • We talk

    You tell me about your project and your audience. I ask questions until the goal is clear.

  • I build a prototype

    You get something to try early on, not just a document.

  • We refine it

    You test it, tell me what works and what doesn't, and I adjust until it's right.

  • I look after it

    After launch, I stay on hand for fixes, updates and new features.
