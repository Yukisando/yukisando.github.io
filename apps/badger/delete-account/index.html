Delete Your Badger Account

Important: deleting your account is permanent and cannot be undone.

Using Badger without an account? Nothing is stored on our side: uninstalling the app removes your card and contacts from your phone.

Delete it in the app

  1. Open Badger and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Tap Delete account.
  3. Enter your password and tap Delete.

Your account and its cloud data are deleted right away.

No access to the app? Ask us by email

Send us an email from the address you signed up with. We delete the account within 7 days and confirm by email.

Email a deletion request

What is deleted

  • Your account (email address and password)
  • All your cloud cards and their photos
  • All contacts you synced to your account

Nothing is kept after deletion.
