Privacy Policy for Patoune

Last updated: February 9, 2026

Summary

Patoune is a small utility app that helps cat owners remember which claws they trimmed recently. The app stores only the minimal information necessary to provide this function (e.g., paw identifiers and dates). We do not collect personal information or analytics.

Data we access

All data created by Patoune is stored locally on your device (in the app's local storage). We do not transmit or back up this information to any server by default.

How we use your data

The data is used only to show the history of trimmed claws and to help you track future trims. The app does not share or sell any of this information.

Third-party services

Patoune does not use third-party analytics, advertising, or cloud services. If you choose to export or back up your data using another service, that action is handled by the tools you select, not by Patoune.

Your rights

You can delete all app data at any time via the app settings or by uninstalling the app. If you have questions or want assistance, contact us at the address below.
