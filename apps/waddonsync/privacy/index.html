Privacy Policy for WaddonSync

Last updated: January 12, 2026

Summary

WaddonSync is a desktop app that stores your addon backups in your Google Drive. We do not collect personal data.

Data we access

The app only accesses files it creates in your Google Drive (a folder named WaddonSync Backups).

How we use your data

Backups are stored in your Google Drive. The app retains the 3 most recent backups; you can delete them anytime from Google Drive.

Third-party services

We use the Google Drive API to store files in your Drive.

Your rights

You can revoke Google Drive access via your Google Account > Security > Manage third-party access. Contact us to delete any hosted content or for questions.
