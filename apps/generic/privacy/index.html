Privacy Policy

Overview

This privacy policy applies to apps and games developed by Nathan De Castro ("we," "us," or "our"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any information related to your use of our applications.

Information We Collect

Our apps and games are designed with privacy in mind. We do not collect, store, or share any personal data. We do not require account registration, and we do not track your activity.

Third-Party Services

Our apps may use third-party services that have their own privacy policies. These may include:

  • Google Play Services — Required for app distribution and updates on Android devices. Google Privacy Policy
  • AdMob (if applicable) — Some free apps may display ads. AdMob may collect device identifiers for ad personalization. AdMob Privacy Policy

If an app uses additional services, they will be disclosed within the app or its store listing.

Children's Privacy

Our apps do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will take steps to delete such information.

Data Storage

Any data generated by the app (such as game progress or settings) is stored locally on your device. We do not have access to this data. If you uninstall the app, this local data may be deleted.

Your Rights

Since we do not collect personal data, there is no personal information to access, modify, or delete. If an app uses cloud services, you can manage your data through those service providers directly.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
