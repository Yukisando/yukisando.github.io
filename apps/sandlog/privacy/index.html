Privacy Policy for Sandlog

Last updated: April 28, 2026

Summary

Sandlog is a dream journal app. Your dream entries are stored locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or share your personal data.

Data we collect

We do not collect any personal information. Dream entries, tags, and settings are kept solely on your device and are never sent to our servers.

Analytics

The app does not use any third-party analytics or advertising services.

Your rights

Because no data leaves your device, you remain in full control. You can delete all data at any time by uninstalling the app. Contact us for any questions.
